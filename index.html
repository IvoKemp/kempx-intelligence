The problem

Everything is fragmented.

Your tools don't talk to each other. Your AI forgets everything when the chat ends. Decisions disappear into threads and documents nobody opens again. Goals live in someone's head.

So organisations repeat mistakes, move slower than they should and rarely have a complete picture of where they stand or what needs deciding next.

What's missing isn't another tool. It's a layer.

Somewhere in your organisation, a decision is being made that contradicts one made six months ago. Nobody has noticed. A layer that remembered both would have flagged it weeks before it cost you.

Context is scattered.

Each tool holds a fragment. None knows what the others hold, or why any of it matters.

AI starts from zero.

Every new conversation begins without your goals, your history or the reasoning behind past decisions.

Nothing is watching.

No system compares today's work with what was decided last quarter. Drift is found in the post-mortem.

How it works

Four systems. One picture.

Everything KempX does comes from four systems that share a single model of your organisation. They are not features bolted together. Each one feeds the others.

01

Goal Graph

A living map of what the organisation is trying to achieve, from strategy down to this week's work.

  • Links objectives, sub-goals and the work beneath them
  • Connects decisions to the outcomes they were meant to move
  • Flags work that no longer serves a stated goal
  • Updates as priorities change, without manual upkeep
02

Knowledge Map

Connects documents, conversations, tools and records into one graph of how things relate, rather than a pile of files.

  • Reads from docs, CRM, Slack, Drive, email and code
  • Builds links between people, projects and decisions over time
  • Brings the right context to the moment it's needed
  • Shows where knowledge is duplicated or missing
03

Decision Memory

Every decision is kept with its reasoning, the alternatives considered and the assumptions behind it.

  • Records who decided, when and why
  • Links outcomes back to the decisions that caused them
  • Stops the same mistake recurring next quarter
  • Keeps institutional memory when people leave
04

Signal Engine

Watches for gaps, risks and misalignment, and raises them before anyone thinks to ask.

  • Raises signals, not reports
  • Spots strategic drift and work that conflicts with decisions
  • Finds missing context and decisions nobody owns
  • Learns which signals matter to you

Why now

Models are converging. Context isn't.

AI models keep getting better and cheaper, and the gap between the best of them keeps narrowing. What none of them has is your organisation: its goals, its history and the reasoning behind its decisions. That is where lasting value sits, and it belongs in a layer you own, above whichever models you use.

01

Models are interchangeable

Switching from one model to another gets easier every quarter. Building on any single one is a short-term bet.

02

Context is the scarce part

A model can reason about anything. It can only reason well about your business if it knows your goals and decisions.

03

Memory compounds

Every decision recorded and every signal confirmed makes the next one sharper. The value grows with use and is hard to copy.
